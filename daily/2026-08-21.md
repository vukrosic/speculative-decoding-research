  exact acceptance.

  accuracy, validation acceptance, exactness, and throughput can move in
  different directions.

  pass exact target-equivalence.

## Decisions

  excluded.
- Failures receive permanent experiment cards and index entries.
- Results are promoted by the metric matching the actual question, with
  correctness before speed claims.
