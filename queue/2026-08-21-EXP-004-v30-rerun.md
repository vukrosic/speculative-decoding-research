## Interpretation / decision

Q4_K_M was the fastest drafter arm by 0.044 tok/s over F16 and 0.017 tok/s
over Q8_0, while all three remained about 33% below target-only and had the
same 7/12 target-content match count. This is a bounded approximate negative
result for a meaningful serving improvement; it does not establish quality,
exactness, losslessness, or general speed. Preserve the existing quantization
proposal as historical evidence, but do not promote a new quantization choice.

The next highest-information planner action is to repair the existing
PLAN-20260821-001 evaluator/layout contract (or keep it blocked), rather than
repeat v30 export formats. Existing PQ-004 adaptive proposal-length and other
planner proposals remain unchanged and unauthorized.
