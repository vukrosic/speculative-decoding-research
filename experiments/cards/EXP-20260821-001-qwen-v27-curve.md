# Which training point and proposal length maximize custom-Qwen acceptance?

## Frozen controls

- Target: Qwen3.8-27B IQ1_S, target layer 62.
- Drafter: selector-free compact DFlash block-17, 16 trained positions,
  47,206,656 total/trainable parameters.
- Data: frozen 9,944 train / 508 source-disjoint validation windows; held-out
  sealed.
- Features: v23b compact target features. Labels: v23f hard successor tokens
  and frozen target output head.
- Objective: position-weighted hard-label token CE, gamma 8, label smoothing
  0.02; no target-logit distillation.
- AdamW 1e-4, betas 0.9/0.95, weight decay 0.01, batch 2, seed 42, BF16.
- Baseline parent: v23f epoch-03 token-CE checkpoint.

## Observed

| Proposals | Selected checkpoint | Accepted/proposed | Acceptance | Accepted/cycle | Tok/s |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | curve step 4000 | 102/1,971 | 5.175% | 0.20198 | 11.476 |
| 8 | curve step 3200 | 105/3,804 | 2.760% | 0.20916 | 9.820 |
| 16 | epoch 04 | 105/7,137 | 1.471% | 0.20958 | 8.597 |

Target-only on the same validation screen was 17.592 tok/s. Target-content
matches were 7/12, 5/12, and 7/12 for n=4/8/16 respectively, so no exactness or
speedup claim is supported.

Training-step acceptance was non-monotonic. For n=4: steps 800/1600/2400/3200/
4000 accepted 95/101/93/101/102 tokens respectively.

## Interpretation / hypotheses

n=4 is the practical custom-drafter validation incumbent. Selecting only by CE
or final epoch would miss length-specific acceptance optima. The small screen is
underpowered and approximate.
