# Does training only positions 1–4 improve n=4 acceptance?

## Protocol

Starting from v27 curve step 4000, apply equal hard-label CE to positions 1–4
and zero loss weight to positions 5–16 for one continuation epoch. Target,
architecture (47,206,656 parameters), data, optimizer family, seed, runtime, and
validation screen remained fixed.

## Observed

- Validation mean token accuracy fell from 48.38% to 45.32%.
- n=4 acceptance fell from 102/1,971 = 5.175% to 96/1,992 = 4.819%.
- Accepted/cycle fell from 0.20198 to 0.18787.
- Throughput fell from 11.476 to 11.347 tok/s.
- Target-content matches remained 7/12; exactness did not pass.

## Interpretation / hypotheses

Early-position-only supervision is not sufficient. Later-position training may
regularize shared representations or maintain coherent block interactions even
when serving with n=4.
