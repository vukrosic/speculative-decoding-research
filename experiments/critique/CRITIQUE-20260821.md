# Experiment critique — 2026-08-21

This is a critique-owned review of the evidence currently indexed in this
repository. It does not alter experiment cards, receipts, results, literature
records, or the research queue. No experiment, download, evaluation, or
external action was run.

## Bottom line

The repository supports several useful *bounded observations*: teacher-forced
metrics can move independently of acceptance; the published Qwen runtime was
not strict-greedy exact on the recorded prompts; the five-layer SmolLM
candidate improved acceptance but lost end-to-end throughput on its fixed
screen; and the compact Qwen drafter has a narrow validation-screen incumbent.

It does not yet support broad claims about the best drafter, lossless serving,
general workload transfer, or the causal effect of capacity, alignment, or an
objective. The most important blockers are exposed/overused evaluation screens,
confounded ablations, and missing compact receipts for most external results.

## Severity scale

- **Critical** — invalidates the stated causal or deployment claim until
  repaired; the observation may remain useful as a bounded diagnostic.
- **High** — materially weakens promotion or cross-experiment comparison.
- **Medium** — limits precision, reproducibility, or generalization but does not
  erase the local observation.
- **Low** — presentation or bookkeeping issue with little effect on the local
  result.

## Facts and evidence

### F-01 — Receipt and provenance closure is incomplete (**High**)

**Facts.** Only EXP-20260821-006 has a compact receipt in this checkout.
The other cards point to external benchmark files, scripts, checkpoints, or
logs. EXP-20260819-001 leaves checkpoint and receipt hashes to be backfilled;
EXP-20260820-001 leaves the published drafter hash and exact flags to an
external receipt; and the Qwen v27–v31 cards reference external experiment
roots. Driver/CUDA are explicitly `unknown` for the early SmolLM runs, while
Qwen build/compiler/runtime details are not reproduced in the compact records.

**Evidence.** [EXP-20260819-001](../cards/EXP-20260819-001-smollm-onpolicy20k.md),
[EXP-20260820-001](../cards/EXP-20260820-001-qwen-published-dflash-runtime.md),
[EXP-20260821-001](../cards/EXP-20260821-001-qwen-v27-curve.md),
[EXP-20260821-005](../cards/EXP-20260821-005-qwen-v31-block17-alignment.md),
and the receipt requirements in [`receipts/README.md`](../../receipts/README.md).

### F-02 — SmolLM test/held-out material is not sealed (**Critical**)

**Facts.** EXP-20260819-001 and EXP-20260819-002 declare
`heldout_touched: true`. The same records describe an 18k/1k/1k train,
validation, and test split, report test loss/accuracy or full-test acceptance,
and use a fixed 90-prompt acceptance/exactness screen. The cards do not state
whether the 90 prompts overlap the 1k test set, so that overlap is `unknown`.
The 1k test and full-test measurements are therefore exposed evidence, not a
sealed final holdout.

**Evidence.** [EXP-20260819-001](../cards/EXP-20260819-001-smollm-onpolicy20k.md)
and [EXP-20260819-002](../cards/EXP-20260819-002-smollm-capacity35m.md).

### F-03 — Qwen validation selection is repeatedly reused and underpowered for promotion (**High**)

**Facts.** EXP-20260821-001 searches several training steps and proposal
lengths (n=4/8/16) on the same 508-window validation set and selects the best
screen point. EXP-20260821-005 searches neighboring epochs and proposal
lengths on a 152-window validation set. EXP-20260821-002 and EXP-20260821-003
are then judged against the v27 validation incumbent. Held-out data is marked
sealed, but the validation screen is a repeatedly optimized selection set.
The cards provide no multiplicity correction, confidence interval, or
prompt-level bootstrap.

**Evidence.** [EXP-20260821-001](../cards/EXP-20260821-001-qwen-v27-curve.md),
[EXP-20260821-002](../cards/EXP-20260821-002-qwen-v28-small-drafter.md),
[EXP-20260821-003](../cards/EXP-20260821-003-qwen-v29-early4.md), and
[EXP-20260821-005](../cards/EXP-20260821-005-qwen-v31-block17-alignment.md).

### F-04 — Several intended single-variable comparisons are confounded (**High**)

**Facts.**

- EXP-20260821-005 changes feature extraction shape, train/validation corpus
  size (1,536/152 versus v27's 9,944/508), and continues the v27 parent. It is
  not an alignment-only ablation.
- EXP-20260821-006 compares a one-layer checkpoint with a five-layer candidate
  from a different parent; the candidate is identity-initialized and epoch 0
  is selected after later validation loss worsens. The card explicitly calls
  this a capacity/initialization comparison, not pure retraining.
- EXP-20260821-003 changes the loss mask and performs one continuation epoch
  from the v27 selected checkpoint. Objective change, optimization path, and
  training budget are bundled.
- EXP-20260821-002 changes attention heads, KV heads, FFN width, and parameter
  count together. A result cannot be attributed to parameter count alone.

**Evidence.** [EXP-20260821-002](../cards/EXP-20260821-002-qwen-v28-small-drafter.md),
[EXP-20260821-003](../cards/EXP-20260821-003-qwen-v29-early4.md),
[EXP-20260821-005](../cards/EXP-20260821-005-qwen-v31-block17-alignment.md),
and [EXP-20260821-006](../cards/EXP-20260821-006-smollm-1layer-vs-5layer.md).

### F-05 — Exactness evidence is contract- and screen-scoped (**High**)

**Facts.** The published Qwen path fails strict output identity on 4/6
prompts. The custom Qwen cards report target-content matches such as 7/12,
not a formal token-by-token exactness guard. SmolLM EXP-20260819-001 and
EXP-20260821-006 pass their fixed exact comparison, but only on 90 prompts
(180 timed calls for EXP-006); they do not establish general serving
losslessness or a sealed held-out result. EXP-20260821-005's exact anchor/mask
matches coexist with nonzero hidden-state RMSE, so those preflight matches do
not prove output equivalence.

**Evidence.** [EXP-20260820-001](../cards/EXP-20260820-001-qwen-published-dflash-runtime.md),
[EXP-20260821-001](../cards/EXP-20260821-001-qwen-v27-curve.md),
[EXP-20260821-005](../cards/EXP-20260821-005-qwen-v31-block17-alignment.md),
and the exactness contract in [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md).

### F-06 — Speed evidence is uneven and cannot be promoted uniformly (**High**)

**Facts.** EXP-20260820-001 reports 22.4079 versus 18.0908 tok/s, but strict
identity fails; the card correctly labels this an approximate comparison.
Qwen v27–v29 report tok/s on small validation screens without a compact record
of matched warmups, repetitions, latency distribution, or output-token
accounting. EXP-20260821-004 repeats Q8_0 three times but reports only one F16
and one Q4_K_M value, so the 0.65% Q8_0 delta lacks a matched variance
baseline. EXP-20260821-006 has two repetitions and a matched target-only
comparison, but the receipt includes singleton exact-guard calls; whether
those calls are part of normal deployment or instrumentation overhead is not
stated.

**Evidence.** [EXP-20260820-001](../cards/EXP-20260820-001-qwen-published-dflash-runtime.md),
[EXP-20260821-004](../cards/EXP-20260821-004-qwen-v30-quantization.md),
[EXP-20260821-006](../cards/EXP-20260821-006-smollm-1layer-vs-5layer.md), and
[`benchmarks/INDEX.md`](../../benchmarks/INDEX.md).

### F-07 — Acceptance denominators and baselines are not fully comparable (**High**)

**Facts.** The Qwen custom rows use accepted/proposed counts for n=4/8/16,
whereas the published Qwen row reports 245/466 and approximately 3.50 tokens
per verification cycle. The cards do not establish that these denominators,
position accounting, rejection handling, and cycle definitions are identical.
EXP-20260819-002 itself warns that its full-test acceptance is not directly
comparable to the fixed balanced slice. Qwen v28, v29, and v31 omit a fresh
target-only timing baseline in their result tables. The repository also lacks
matched DFlash/EAGLE/Medusa/MTP baselines despite listing them in the research
queue.

**Evidence.** [`results/INDEX.md`](../../results/INDEX.md),
[EXP-20260819-002](../cards/EXP-20260819-002-smollm-capacity35m.md),
[EXP-20260821-001](../cards/EXP-20260821-001-qwen-v27-curve.md), and
[papers/INDEX.md](../../papers/INDEX.md).

### F-08 — The training objectives are proxies for acceptance, not acceptance objectives (**Medium**)

**Facts.** The Qwen custom family uses hard successor-token CE with position
weighting and label smoothing; v29 masks positions 5–16 for one continuation
epoch. SmolLM checkpoint selection is based on validation loss. EXP-20260819-002
shows lower loss/higher teacher-forced accuracy alongside lower acceptance,
and EXP-20260821-001 shows non-monotonic acceptance across training steps.
No card reports a loss explicitly calibrated to contiguous-prefix survival,
accepted tokens per cycle, target-call reduction, or the deployed quantized
verifier state distribution.

**Evidence.** [EXP-20260819-002](../cards/EXP-20260819-002-smollm-capacity35m.md),
[EXP-20260821-001](../cards/EXP-20260821-001-qwen-v27-curve.md),
[EXP-20260821-003](../cards/EXP-20260821-003-qwen-v29-early4.md), and
[`docs/RESEARCH-METHOD.md`](../../docs/RESEARCH-METHOD.md).

### F-09 — Literature evidence is queued but not yet reviewable (**Medium**)

**Facts.** `papers/INDEX.md` contains a reading queue but no paper notes or
versioned author-claim/reproduction mappings. Therefore the repository cannot
yet distinguish what is directly supported by DFlash/DFlash2/EAGLE-family
literature from what is an internal compatibility-bridge observation.

**Evidence.** [`papers/INDEX.md`](../../papers/INDEX.md),
[`README.md`](../../README.md), and
[`runtimes/RUNTIME-llamacpp-dflash-pr27342.md`](../../runtimes/RUNTIME-llamacpp-dflash-pr27342.md).

## Hypotheses and interpretations (not established facts)

These are useful working hypotheses only. None should be written as a result
until a discriminating comparison passes its declared gate.

### H-01 — Quantized verifier shape dependence is a major contributor to Qwen exactness failure (**High**)

The v31 preflight shows materially different singleton/block target states and
the runtime note records shape-dependent IQ1_S trajectories. This makes shape
dependence plausible, but the existing evidence does not isolate it from
cache, mask, correction, numerical, or drafter-interface causes.

### H-02 — Contiguous-prefix survival and verifier-state matching matter more than average CE (**High**)

The capacity, early-position, and alignment results all permit the same
interpretation: average teacher-forced metrics are insufficient predictors of
autoregressive acceptance. The evidence does not yet identify whether the
missing ingredient is calibration, state distribution, position coupling, or
runtime semantics.

### H-03 — Target verification and runtime overhead dominate the small-drafter savings (**Medium**)

The smaller Qwen drafter lost acceptance and throughput, and Q8_0 produced only
a small approximate change. This is consistent with target/shared-head/
launch/cache overhead dominating, but no call-count or component-level timing
breakdown proves the attribution.

### H-04 — The five-layer SmolLM acceptance gain is partly an initialization or
selection effect, not a demonstrated depth effect (**High**)

Because the candidate is identity-initialized from a different parent and epoch
0 is selected, the observed +1.1521 percentage-point acceptance change may be
caused by initialization, checkpoint history, or screen variance. More capacity
could still help under a controlled training protocol; the current comparison
does not decide that question.

### H-05 — The v27 “incumbent” is a validation-screen winner with optimism bias (**High**)

Selecting across steps and proposal lengths makes v27 a reasonable engineering
candidate for that screen, but likely optimistic for a fresh workload. The
sealed held-out result remains unknown, not negative.

### H-06 — The current queue will reproduce the same selection bias unless its gates are executable metadata (**Medium**)

The planner entries name useful controls, but most do not yet carry an
immutable validation manifest, multiplicity budget, or receipt schema that can
be checked before a run. This is a process hypothesis, not a claim that a
queued experiment is invalid; it follows from the missing compact artifacts in
F-01 and the repeated screens in F-03.

## Recommendations and promotion gates

### R-01 — Close receipt gaps before making new cross-experiment claims (**P0**)

Create additive compact receipts for EXP-001 through EXP-005 containing exact
commands, working directories, checkpoint/drafter hashes, evaluator hashes,
dataset/split hashes, output-token counts, warmups/repetitions, latency
distribution, peak VRAM, and final process state. Preserve `unknown` where the
source evidence cannot be recovered. Do not copy large artifacts into Git.

### R-02 — Reclassify exposed SmolLM test evidence (**P0**)

Treat the 1k test and any overlapping fixed-90 screen in EXP-001/002 as
exposed evaluation evidence. Keep those observations, but do not use them for
future selection or call them final held-out results. Record the 90-prompt
overlap status explicitly; if it cannot be proven, leave it `unknown`.

### R-03 — Freeze a fresh selection protocol for Qwen (**P0**)

Declare the existing 508/152-window screens as development-only, record the
number of checkpoint × proposal-length looks, and promote exactly once on a
new sealed split. Report prompt-level uncertainty and a predeclared primary
metric. A held-out result must remain untouched until the candidate and n are
frozen.

### R-04 — Repair causal isolation in alignment, objective, and depth studies (**P1**)

For an alignment claim, hold corpus size, parent checkpoint, optimizer budget,
and selection rule fixed while changing only feature shape. For a depth claim,
use the same parent/state and a predeclared equal training budget (or label the
result initialization-only). For an objective claim, equalize continuation
steps and compare against a same-step control. These are design requirements,
not requests to run work in this critique.

### R-05 — Standardize acceptance and exactness accounting (**P1**)

Every receipt should report per-position acceptance, contiguous-prefix survival,
accepted tokens per verification cycle, target/draft call counts, EOS and
rejection/correction behavior, and the exact denominator. Use a formal
token-by-token oracle for exactness; keep target-content match counts in a
separate approximate-quality field. Batch-one replay, fixed-screen identity,
and production serving losslessness must have different labels.

### R-06 — Apply a complete speed gate (**P1**)

Require matched target-only and speculative runs under the same runtime,
hardware, prompt order, output-token budget, warmup, and repeated timing plan.
Report mean/median/p95 and per-prompt variation. Repeat both candidate and
baseline, not only one quantization arm. State whether exact-guard calls are
normal serving work or instrumentation; otherwise do not call the measurement
production end-to-end speed.

### R-07 — Align objectives with the actual promotion metric (**P1**)

Keep CE and token accuracy as diagnostics, but predeclare a promotion metric
such as contiguous accepted tokens per cycle under the deployed verifier and
quality/exactness contract. Candidate objective families should explicitly
test verifier-state matching, position coupling, and calibrated/distilled
targets rather than interpreting CE improvements as acceptance evidence.

### R-08 — Add literature notes before claiming method reproduction (**P2**)

For each queued method, record the exact paper/code version, authors' contract,
baseline, data, verifier semantics, and which repository observation reproduces
or fails to reproduce it. Keep the local compatibility bridge visibly separate
from an official implementation.

### R-09 — Clarify incumbent scope and ties (**P2**)

The 10.13M two-layer SmolLM bridge and the 20.96M five-layer candidate both
report 7.1566% on their fixed screens, but only the latter has the bounded
timing comparison. Results should name the metric, evaluator, prompt slice,
exactness contract, and throughput status whenever calling something an
“incumbent”; do not collapse acceptance ties into a single global winner.

### R-10 — Make queue gates machine-checkable before handoff (**P2**)

Keep the queue's `CEO DECISION REQUIRED` boundary. Before moving any item to
`ready-needs-approval`, attach the exact validation manifest/hash, the number
of allowed checkpoint/proposal looks, the held-out seal statement, the matched
baseline receipt, and the terminal-receipt schema. For PLAN-003 specifically,
label the 90-prompt result as a development screen until a fresh sealed split
is available; for PLAN-001, recover the missing published drafter hash and
historical flags before treating the probe as reproducible.

## Claims that should remain explicitly unsupported

- No exact Qwen DFlash speedup or lossless Qwen runtime is established.
- No broad claim that larger, smaller, aligned, or early-position-only capacity
  is intrinsically better or worse is established.
- No teacher-forced-loss-to-acceptance monotonicity is established.
- No Qwen custom-drafter held-out claim is established.
- No cross-workload, cross-hardware, or production-SLO ranking is established.
- No literature reproduction claim is established from the queue alone.
