# 2026-08-21 literature handoff — PQ-20260821-004 proposal length

## Scope and verdict

PQ-004 asks whether a pre-registered confidence gate choosing `n∈{1,2,3,4}`
can improve the Qwen acceptance/overhead trade-off over fixed `n=4`. **Verdict:
conditionally high-information and bounded as a runtime-only screen; not an
execution approval.** The policy must be frozen before validation replay and
must remain separate from PLAN-001's verifier/state contract.

## Observed local evidence

The current v30 rerun measured fixed Q8_0 `n=4` at **11.5572 tok/s**,
102/1971 accepted/proposed, and 7/12 target-content matches on each timed
repetition, versus **17.3758 tok/s** target-only. Exactness was failed
diagnostically. This establishes an approximate baseline and leaves room to
test draft-overhead scheduling, but does not establish that adaptive length can
recover the gap.

## Hypothesis and falsifiers

**Hypothesis:** weak prefixes can stop after fewer than four proposals, reducing
wasted drafting and target verification while retaining most of fixed-`n=4`
accepted tokens. Adaptive overhead or quantized confidence miscalibration may
instead erase the savings.

**Falsifiers:** the frozen gate changes prompt order or cache/correction
semantics; target-only baseline fails; accepted/cycle or repeated timing is no
better than fixed `n=4`; or the policy cannot account for every chosen length
and target verification.

## Boundary conditions

- One changed variable: proposal scheduling policy only. No retraining,
  export-format change, target/runtime change, evaluator change, or drafter
  mutation.
- Twelve source-disjoint validation prompts; no held-out selection. One fixed
  threshold; no repeated threshold search.
- Repeat timing only after the single policy replay meets the pre-registered
  acceptance/cycle guard. Preserve target-only baseline and cleanup receipt.
- Acceptance, target-content, quality, timing, and exactness remain separate.
  If strict identity fails, report approximate evidence only; never use
  `exact`, `lossless`, `equivalent`, or “quality preserved.”
- The result cannot resolve PLAN-001's singleton/block state mismatch and must
  not be used as a verifier-fidelity or general serving claim.

## Recommendation

Keep PQ-004 as a high-information bounded follow-up after the v30 baseline,
provided the confidence function, threshold source, chosen-length logging, and
shape/cache invariants are frozen in a new receipt schema. Compare against the
new 11.5572 tok/s Q8_0 receipt, not the older 11.550 historical estimate.
