# Literature verdict — PQ-20260821-002 acceptance-calibrated objective

Date: 2026-08-21  
Verdict: **Bounded design, but not the highest-information next Qwen action**

## Observed local evidence

- v27 n=4 is the incumbent at 102/1,971 = 5.175% acceptance; the v29
  early-positions-only continuation fell to 96/1,992 = 4.819%.
- The v30 Q8_0 serving rerun remained approximate (102/1,971, 11.5572 tok/s,
  7/12 target-content matches) and slower than its target-only baseline.
- These results show that teacher-forced/early-position emphasis has not yet
  demonstrated an autoregressive acceptance gain, and runtime overhead remains
  material.

## Hypothesis and falsifiers

**Hypothesis:** weighting early positions while retaining all-position
supervision, plus a prefix-survival penalty, may improve first-position and
contiguous-prefix acceptance over v27 without relying on CE as a proxy.

**Falsifiers:** finite-fit failure; CE/accuracy gains with prefix/acceptance
regression; no improvement over v27 at n=4/8/16; or gains that disappear under
the frozen block-shaped target state. A target-content match is not a quality
or exactness falsifier.

## Information and boundary verdict

PQ-002 is a valid bounded *training* design if the formula, parent checkpoint,
feature/label manifests, optimizer, seed, and one-checkpoint selection rule are
frozen before fitting. It is not the highest-information next Qwen action while
PLAN-001's verifier/state mismatch remains unresolved: a better drafter may
still fail the same runtime exactness boundary, making the causal result hard
to interpret. Prioritize the target-only singleton/block localization first;
retain PQ-002 as a follow-up if that gate passes or is explicitly deferred.

No training, acceptance, quality, exactness, or speed claim is authorized by
this literature verdict. Held-out data must remain sealed, and any selected
candidate remains validation-only until the separate correctness gate passes.
