# EAGLE-3: Scaling up Inference Acceleration via Training-Time Test

**Citation:** Yuhui Li, Fangyun Wei, Chao Zhang, Hongyang Zhang, NeurIPS 2025,
[arXiv:2503.01840](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01840).

## Mechanism

**Verified fact:** EAGLE-3 replaces EAGLE's feature-prediction constraint with
direct token prediction, fuses features from multiple target layers, and uses
training-time test to simulate multi-step drafting during training.

## Claims and evidence

**Author claim:** the paper reports up to 6.5x speedup and about 1.4x the
EAGLE-2 result; its SGLang study reports 1.38x throughput improvement at batch
64.

**Weak point:** the method requires target hidden-state access and additional
feature extraction. Training-time test changes the training cost and is not a
guarantee of serving speed on another backend.

## Mapping

Record the extracted layer IDs, fusion projection, draft steps, tree policy,
and target runtime separately. Treat the paper's speedup as an author claim
until a matched local target-only baseline is measured.
